🍳 Kitchen

Scan barcodes to add items to the kitchen.

Scans are looked up in Open Food Facts, then saved straight into the shared inventory. Re-scanning an item bumps its quantity.

Check items off as you buy them, then "Bought" moves them into the kitchen inventory (or scan them in for full details). The ✕ dismisses an item without buying it.
